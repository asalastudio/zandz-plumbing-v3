## Direction in one sentence

Z and Z is the serious East Bay plumbing and infrastructure contractor other plumbers call when the job is too big or too licensed for them. Home Depot / Husky / DeWalt lineage: Hero Orange + Black + White, refined faucet mark, Barlow Condensed display, Inter UI, black trust strip. No heritage tones. No mascots. Contractor-grade, highly legible, built for trucks, uniforms, signage, search, and same-day conversion.

## Locked decisions

- **Tagline (primary):** The Pros Other Plumbers Call
- **Tagline (supporting):** Two licenses. One crew. Same-day service.
- **Primary color:** Hero Orange `#F96302`
- **Logo wordmark:** `Z AND Z PLUMBING`, spelled out, all caps, no ampersand
- **Typography:** Barlow Condensed for display/logo/headlines, Inter for body/UI
- **Founding year:** 2003 (not 1985, do not use)
- **HQ:** 3057 Teagarden Street, San Leandro, CA 94577
- **License framing:** C-36 plus A General Engineering, CSLB #896116
- **Mascot:** Deferred to Phase 2
- **Photography:** Existing Z and Z assets for v1, planned shoot Phase 2

## Banned forever

- Prussian Blue, Warm Gold, Bone, any heritage tones
- The rescinded `Z & Z PLUMBING` ampersand wordmark
- "Old House Specialists" positioning
- "Since 1985" or any pre-2003 founding date claims
- Em-dashes in any customer-facing copy
- "Look no further," "in today's fast-paced world," "we pride ourselves on," "state-of-the-art," "top-notch," "unparalleled"
- Cartoonish mascots
- Oakland-first framing (Oakland is the largest market; San Leandro is HQ)
