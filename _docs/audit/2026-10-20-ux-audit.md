# Z and Z OS Usability Audit, 2026-10-20

**Method:** walked every back-office screen in a browser as Jay (desktop at 1440px and phone at 375px) and the field app as a tech, signed in on localhost against the live database. Read-only: no lead, job, or invoice was changed. The public site was audited separately (section 3).

---

## 1. Back office: what was hard to use, and what changed

### Working leads (Jay's main job)

| Problem found | Why it mattered | Fixed |
|---|---|---|
| The lead list had no received date or wait time | A lead from this morning looked the same as one from August; nothing told Jay what to call first | "Received" column ("12 min", "3 h", "Aug 14"); new leads waiting more than one business day get a red edge and an "Overdue" tag |
| No phone, city, or ZIP on the list; the only row action was a red Delete | Jay had to open every lead to call it; deleting was easier than calling | Tap-to-call phone, city, and ZIP on every row; delete moved to a small grey icon |
| Contact-page leads were titled "Contact page request" | 31 of 62 leads were indistinguishable | First 80 characters of the customer's message under the title; detail page titled "Web request from <name>" |
| On a phone, nine filter buttons filled the first screen and leads were a sideways-scrolling table | The phone is where Jay checks leads between calls | Stacked lead cards with a full-width Call button; filters in one swipeable row |
| On the lead page, the customer's message was at the very bottom, labelled "Customer-facing notes"; no received time; raw phone (+16474503554) | Jay couldn't see what was wrong before calling | Summary card at the top: name, tap-to-call phone, email, location, the customer's own words, received time and wait, source page, preferred callback time, out-of-area warning |
| A new lead's only move was "Cancelled"; there was no way to record "I called" without booking a time | Escalation emails kept firing after Jay called; response time could never be measured | **Log call** (records first contact, stops the alerts, starts response-time tracking) and **Spam** (cancels with a note); a "Called" badge follows the lead everywhere |
| Dashboard said "52 new leads" and "2 leads still not contacted" at once | Looked like a bug; hid the real backlog | "52 new leads never called, oldest waited 87 days", with recent missed callbacks shown separately |
| Dispatch showed only booked jobs | The 52 leads that needed booking were invisible from the board | "Waiting to schedule" panel on the board, plus a date picker |
| Header ticker showed "Contact page request" | Same as above | Shows "Web request" |

### Money

| Problem found | Why it mattered | Fixed |
|---|---|---|
| New Invoice had "Send now" and "Email the invoice" ticked by default, but the button just said "Create invoice" | One click emailed the customer without saying so | Button says what it will do ("Create and email invoice" or "Save draft"); "Text the invoice" is disabled until texting is set up |
| Payment picker labelled "Manual" | Unclear what it meant | "Paid by", with "Other" for the catch-all |
| Estimate builder offered "New customer instead" but then refused to save ("Pick a customer first"), losing the line items | A dead end on a common task | New customers are created the same way as on invoices, with the same duplicate guard |

### Customers and history

| Problem found | Why it mattered | Fixed |
|---|---|---|
| A past customer's 2021 to 2023 work didn't show anywhere on their record ("Invoice history (0)") | When a past customer calls, Jay can't see what Z and Z did for them | "Past work" section on every customer page, read from the ServiceTitan archive |
| Lifetime spend and job counts were empty for every imported customer, so Top spenders, the repeat-customer rate (0%), and the dormant list ($0 each) were blank | These are exactly the lists the winter reactivation campaign needs | `scripts/backfill-customer-rollups.mjs` is ready (dry run: 911 customers, $708,100). **Not run yet: it updates live records and needs a yes.** |
| Phone numbers shown as raw E.164 on customers, crew, analytics, and the customer picker; emails forced into capitals | Hard to read and dial by eye | Formatted as (510) 555-0123 everywhere; emails in normal case |
| Crew phone field labelled "Phone (E.164)"; numbers stored in mixed formats; no error shown on a bad save | Lead-alert texts to crew need clean numbers | "Mobile phone", normalized on save, clear error message |
| "Click any row to see the underlying invoices someday, not yet wired" on Analytics | Developer copy in Jay's screen | Removed |

## 3. Public site: lead paths

Tested 14 pages at phone (390px) and desktop (1440px) sizes on the live site, stepping through every form without submitting.

**Working well:** every call link dials (510) 708-4237 (no competitor number anywhere); no sideways scrolling, broken images, or console errors; the booking form catches bad phone numbers, handles out-of-area ZIPs gracefully, keeps SMS consent unticked, and has the right phone keyboards; the licenses and "since 2003" show on every page; sewer pages show real price ranges; pages load fast.

| Problem found | Why it cost leads | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **No call button on the first screen on phones.** The hero Call button was desktop-only, the mobile header had only the menu, and the sticky call bar appeared only after scrolling (off entirely on /book/). On the homepage the first call link was about 6,500px down; on the emergency page, below a long paragraph. | Someone with a leak wants to call, and the only thing on screen was a ZIP box | Fixed in this pass (page agent) |
| **Three different callback promises:** 15 minutes, 30 minutes, and "30 to 60 min", plus "a plumber picks up at 2am" | A missed promise is how a 1-star review starts; none of these was measured | Standardized to "within 30 minutes during business hours (Mon to Fri, 7am to 5pm)"; 24/7 copy now says emergency calls are answered 24/7. **Owner to confirm the 30-minute number**; the OS now measures it |
| "Five-star" headline with five full stars next to 4.6 and 4.5 ratings | Overstated ratings undercut trust | Honest figure from the site's own counts |
| The menu promoted "Current coupons" that led to an empty page | A dead end at the moment of interest | Card only shows when a coupon is live |
| Forms said the request "lands in the Z and Z lead dashboard"; city pages tagged every lead with one default ZIP (some of them PO-box ZIPs) | Internal jargon; wrong ZIPs on leads | Customer language; no default ZIP |
| Booking form required email (even for emergencies); "Emergency" was 6th of 7 options, below the fold; "Water Heater" and "No Hot Water" overlapped; "gas smell" had no safety guidance | Friction and risk on the most urgent path | Emergency first; email optional; one water heater option; "Smell gas? Leave the house first, then call PG&E at 1-800-743-5000 or 911." |
| Confirmation screen and email promised a 15-minute callback | Same as above | Now says 30 minutes, business hours |
| Reviews page showed the same 5 testimonials twice; "Review on Google" opened a search page | Thin social proof | Shown once; button relabelled until the direct review link is available |
| Small bugs: mobile headline dropped "Other"; contact phone number wrapped mid-number; grey "Get Help" button with no hint; text under 12px; a stray white band; two H1s on /book/; CSLB link went to the generic lookup | Polish and accessibility | Fixed in this pass (page agent) |

**Still open (needs the owner or a build):**
