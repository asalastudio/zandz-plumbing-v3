## 1. Show up in Google (SEO)

**The single biggest reason this rebuild is happening.**

**What Google sees on the legacy site (from your real GSC data, last
16 months):**
- 325 total clicks
- 221,300 total impressions
- 0.147% click-through rate (industry baseline for plumbers is 2–3%)
- 67% of clicks are from people searching "Z and Z" by name. The site
  is not winning any non-brand searches.
- 50 of 95 known pages are indexed (53%). 12 pages got de-indexed in a
  single day on May 4, 2026.
- 28 thin blog posts that Google has crawled and rejected.
- 75,800 non-brand impressions sitting on page 2-5 of Google
  (so-called "striking distance"). These are real customers
  searching for plumbing work, finding the legacy site near the top
  of page 2, and never clicking through.

**What the new site does about it:**
- Every page has proper schema markup (LocalBusiness, Plumber, Service,
  FAQPage, BreadcrumbList). Tells Google exactly what business you
  are, what you do, where you serve, what your reviews look like.
- 41 prerendered static pages including 12 service hubs, 10 city pages,
  and 4 priority Oakland service+city pages (Sewer Lateral, Emergency,
  Water Heater, Drain Cleaning).
- 78 redirect rules so every legacy URL Google has indexed lands on
  the right new page. No lost search authority.
- Clean URL structure with trailing slashes, canonical tags, sitemap,
  robots.txt: all the technical fundamentals Google grades on.
- Targeted content for the 22,643 non-brand "sewer lateral" search
  impressions and the 51% of non-brand demand from Oakland.

**Realistic forecast (conservative):**
- Day 30: organic clicks up 3× to ~60/month
- Day 60: ~180/month
- Day 90: 350+/month, a 10× lift on what the legacy site delivers
- Annualized revenue impact at $600 average ticket × 10% close rate:
  $180,000–$270,000 of net-new revenue Year 1 from organic search alone.

## 11. Review-velocity engine (Z and Z OS Phase 1)

**This category did not exist before. It's net-new.**

**What it does:**
- HubSpot Workflow → Deal stage moves to "Closed Won" → Webhook fires.
- Webhook receiver validates the HubSpot signature, looks up the
  customer's phone and SMS consent flag.
- If customer opted in, schedules a review-request SMS for +48 hours,
  clamped to an 11am–6pm Pacific window.
- Vercel Cron runs hourly, sends due requests via Twilio Messaging
  Service.
- SMS body is brand-voiced, includes the click-tracked Google review
  link, and includes the legally-required STOP-opt-out instruction.
- Customer taps the link → click is recorded → 302 redirect to
  Google review form.
- If customer replies STOP, they're added to the opt-out list and
  any pending requests are cancelled.

**Compliance:**
- TCPA-compliant consent collection via the HubSpot booking form
  (checkbox: "OK to text me about my service").
- A2P 10DLC carrier registration documented step-by-step in
  WIRING.md.
- HMAC signature validation on the inbound HubSpot webhook.
- Twilio request signature validation on the inbound SMS webhook.

**Expected impact:**
- Industry data: well-tuned post-job SMS drives 25–40% review
  conversion vs ~2–5% organic.
- At Z and Z's current call volume (50–100 jobs/month), that's
  10–40 new Google reviews per month, totally automated.
- Going from 19 Google reviews to 50+ over the first quarter, then
  100+, then on a steady upward curve. Map-pack ranking directly
  correlates with review velocity in the local pack algorithm.

**Cost:** ~$15–20/month all-in (Twilio number + A2P brand fees + per-SMS
fees). Compared to ServiceTitan Marketing Pro: hundreds per month.

## 12. Future-proofing

**Legacy site:**
- WordPress + Beaver Builder + Astra theme. Each component maintained
  by a separate company with their own release cycles, security
  patches, breaking changes, and end-of-life timelines.
- PHP runtime locked to whatever SiteGround supports.
- Asset pipeline depends on plugins that may be abandoned.
- Vendor lock-in: switching out Beaver Builder later means rebuilding
  every page.

**New site:**
- Next.js 16, React 19, TypeScript 5, Tailwind v4: current versions
  of mainstream open-source frameworks. Long support windows.
- Hosted on Vercel (founded by the creators of Next.js, first-class
  platform support).
- All content in version control. Every change traceable.
- No vendor lock-in. The codebase can be migrated off Vercel or
  Supabase if ever necessary. The data export path is clean.
- The Z and Z OS admin is built on the same modern stack as the
  marketing site, so as the operational tools grow, they share the
  same foundation.
