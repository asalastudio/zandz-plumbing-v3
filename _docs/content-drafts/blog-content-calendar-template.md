## Section 2 — Brief (this becomes the writing prompt)

### The reader's situation
Describe the reader in two sentences. What just happened? What do they need to know right now?

### The single question this post answers
One sentence. If you can't write it cleanly, the post isn't focused enough.

### The angle Z & Z brings that competitors don't
Pick one:
- The double-license differentiator (C-36 + A General Engineering)
- 23 years of East Bay-specific experience
- In-house equipment most plumbers sub out (hydrojet, trenchless, camera)
- Honest pricing in an undercutting market
- Real Oakland neighborhood specificity (housing era, foundation type, etc.)

### The required local detail
No generic content. Every post must include at least one of these:
- A specific neighborhood (Rockridge, Montclair, Temescal, Fruitvale, etc.)
- A housing era or building type that matters (pre-1960 galvanized; mid-century cast iron; Victorian-era waste lines)
- A regulatory hook (EBMUD PSL, Oakland green building code, Berkeley sewer lateral requirements)
- A real concrete example or job type the techs see often

### Banned approaches
- No "ultimate guide" intros that summarize what's coming next — get into the content
- No "in today's modern world" filler
- No three-word bulleted lists where every bullet has the same rhythm
- No fabricated case studies. Use only what we know from real jobs.

## Section 5 — Pre-publish checklist

### SEO
- [ ] Primary keyword in title (≤60 chars), URL, H1, first 100 words, one H2
- [ ] Meta description ≤155 chars, includes the keyword once, ends with a CTA
- [ ] Internal links: at least 3 to other Z & Z pages (city hubs, service pages, related posts)
- [ ] External links: at least 1 authoritative source (EBMUD, CSLB, building code, etc.) — opens in new tab
- [ ] FAQ schema implemented if FAQ section exists (Rank Math)
- [ ] Featured image with descriptive filename and alt text
- [ ] Body images have descriptive filenames and alt text — no `IMG_2453.jpg` filenames

### Voice
- [ ] Reads like a master-tradesperson explaining things — not a marketing voice
- [ ] No banned phrases (see brain Section 9)
- [ ] License # visible somewhere on the page (or in the footer site-wide)
- [ ] Phone number with `tel:` link on the page
- [ ] At least one real local detail (neighborhood, era, regulation, example)

### Technical
- [ ] Mobile preview is clean (no paragraph >4 lines on phone width)
- [ ] PageSpeed Insights mobile score >75
- [ ] No 404 internal links
- [ ] Rank Math SEO score green
- [ ] Date is current; if updating an old post, change the modified date

### Distribution
- [ ] Add to GBP as a post in the same week
- [ ] Cross-post a 150-word teaser on Facebook + Nextdoor
- [ ] Add to the email newsletter pipeline if relevant
- [ ] Add to the relevant ServiceTitan customer cohort if it's a job-type-specific post

## Cadence

- Weeks 1–8: foundation (no blog yet — site fixes, GBP, citations, page builds)
- Week 9: first 2 blog posts (problem-aware + ready-to-hire pair)
- Week 10–12: 1 blog post per week
- Month 4+: 2 blog posts per week if capacity allows; otherwise hold at 1/week

The blog is a multiplier — it works best AFTER the foundation is fixed (city pages, schema, GBP optimization). Don't start blogging while the homepage is still showing 9-5 hours in schema and `/contact/` is broken.
