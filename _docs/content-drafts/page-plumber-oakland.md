### Opening paragraph (~110 words)

If you're looking for a plumber in Oakland, you've probably got a problem that won't wait. A backed-up sewer line. A pipe that's leaking behind a wall. A water heater that quit overnight. Call us at [(510) 708-4237](tel:5107084237) for same-day service.

### H2 · Why Oakland homeowners call us first (~180 words)

Oakland's housing stock is older than most of the East Bay. Pre-1960s bungalows in Rockridge, Victorian-era homes in West Oakland, mid-century ranch homes in the Dimond District and Maxwell Park, and post-war foothill houses in Montclair and Piedmont Pines. Every one of these eras has its own plumbing reality:

- Pre-1960s homes almost always have galvanized supply lines that are now 60+ years past their expected life. Low pressure, brown water, pinhole leaks. The fix is a whole-house repipe in copper or PEX.
- Victorian-era homes have original cast iron drain stacks and sewer laterals that fail under pressure or hydrojetting if they're already compromised. Repair-versus-replace decisions matter.
- Mid-century and post-war homes have plumbing layouts that pre-date modern code. A simple bathroom remodel can trigger a chain of upgrades.

We've worked on every one of these. We know which neighborhoods are due for sewer lateral compliance. We know which streets the Oakland Public Works inspectors are walking right now. We bring the trenchless equipment, the commercial-grade hydrojetter, and both license classifications in one truck, so the answer is almost never "we'll have to schedule a second crew."

### H2 · The Oakland services we do most (~280 words with sub-H3s)

#### Sewer lateral repair and EBMUD compliance
The Oakland sewer lateral compliance ordinance triggers at point-of-sale and during major renovations. A failed lateral can stop your real estate transaction cold. We hold the A General Engineering license that legally lets us work past the property line, into the right-of-way and the city main connection. Most Oakland plumbers don't have this license, which means they sub-contract the street-side portion of your job to someone else and you pay two markups. We don't. [More on sewer lateral compliance →](/sewer-lateral-oakland/)

#### Whole-house repipe (galvanized to copper or PEX)
If your Oakland home was built before 1960 and you've never repiped, you have galvanized supply lines. They're failing. The fix is a copper or PEX repipe done minimally invasive, with most walls opened only where access requires it. Typical Oakland repipe runs $15,000–$40,000 depending on square footage, number of bathrooms, and access conditions. [More on repipes →](/repipe-oakland/)

#### Hydrojetting (commercial-grade, in-house)
We own our hydrojetter outright. Restaurant grease lines on Telegraph and Grand. HOA main lines in mid-rise condos near Lake Merritt. Restaurant chains that have multiple Oakland locations. When an auger snake won't clear it, a commercial hydrojet will. [More on hydrojetting →](/hydrojetting-oakland/)

#### Water heater repair and tankless installation
Standard tank, gas, electric, or tankless. We carry common Bay Area inventory in the truck, so most repairs and many replacements happen same-visit. Tankless conversions in Oakland's older homes typically require gas line and venting upgrades, which we handle in-house. [More on water heaters →](/water-heater-oakland/)

#### 24/7 emergency plumbing
Burst pipe at 2 AM in Glenview? Sewer backing up in the laundry room in West Oakland on a Sunday morning? We answer the phone. We dispatch a licensed plumber, not a "service technician" who tells you they need to schedule a return visit for the actual work. [Call (510) 708-4237 →](tel:5107084237)

## 8 · Oakland-specific FAQs (with FAQPage schema)

Render these as a clean expanding/accordion FAQ section. Schema implementation in §10.

**Q: Do I need a sewer lateral compliance certificate to sell my Oakland home?**
A: Yes, in most cases. The Oakland sewer lateral compliance ordinance requires a certificate at point-of-sale for properties not currently certified. The certificate is valid for 20 years. We handle the inspection, repair work if needed, and certificate filing end-to-end. Typical timeline: 7–14 business days from first call to filed certificate.

**Q: How do I know if I have galvanized plumbing in my Oakland house?**
A: If your home was built before 1960 and has never been repiped, you almost certainly have galvanized supply lines. Visual cues: dull gray pipe (not shiny copper) at exposed connections under sinks or at the water heater, low water pressure especially when multiple fixtures run at once, brown or rusty water after a vacation. We do free in-home assessments to confirm.

**Q: Do you really answer the phone at 2 AM in Oakland?**
A: Yes. Our after-hours line goes to a person, not a voicemail. If it's an emergency (active leak, sewer backup, no water, gas smell), we dispatch a licensed plumber, not a triage agent. After-hours rates apply for emergency dispatch, quoted before we roll a truck.

**Q: How long does a whole-house repipe take in an older Oakland home?**
A: For a typical 2-bathroom Oakland bungalow or Victorian, 3–5 working days. For larger homes or homes with finished basements where access is harder, 5–7 days. We minimize wall opening (typically 4-by-4-inch access patches at strategic points, not full wall removal) and you stay in the home throughout. Most rooms have water back within hours of the daily shut-off.

**Q: Are you licensed and bonded in California?**
A: Yes. California Contractors State License Board #896116. We hold both the C-36 plumbing classification (since 2007) and the A General Engineering classification (since 2012). We carry general liability insurance and workers compensation. License verification: [cslb.ca.gov](https://www.cslb.ca.gov).

**Q: What's the difference between a C-36 plumber and one with both C-36 and A General Engineering?**
A: C-36 covers plumbing inside your property. A General Engineering covers civil work in the public right-of-way, like the section of sewer lateral that runs from your property line to the city main, or any plumbing work that involves excavation in the street. Most plumbing companies only hold C-36. When a job crosses the property line, they have to sub-contract the right-of-way portion to a separate company. We do both with one crew, which saves time, coordination friction, and one layer of markup.

**Q: Do you do plumbing work in Oakland's older Victorian and Edwardian homes?**
A: That's most of what we do. Pre-1920 homes have cast iron drain stacks, lead-jointed connections, and original sewer laterals that are well past their service life. We've worked on Victorians in West Oakland and Edwardians in Rockridge for over 20 years. We know how to repair without unnecessary demolition and when full replacement is the right call.
