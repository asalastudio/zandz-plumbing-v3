# Z & Z Plumbing — Voice of Customer Template

**Purpose:** Mine Yelp + Google reviews to extract the actual language Z & Z's customers use. That language becomes the source for homepage hero copy, service-page intros, FAQ entries, ad copy, and the review-request script.

**How to populate:** Pull the 50 most recent + 50 most-engaged Yelp reviews of Z & Z, plus the same sets for the top 3 competitors (Oakland Rooter, Albert Nahman, Mr. Rooter). Also pull all GBP reviews once we have admin access.
